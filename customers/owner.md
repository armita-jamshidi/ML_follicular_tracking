# Owner (builder and buyer)

**Armita Jamshidi.** Wants hands-on experience applying neural networks and kNN to medical
imaging, with a rigorous, honest evaluation to show in a portfolio or class.

What "done" looks like for the owner:
- Can explain every step (why dedup, why grouped folds, why macro-F1, why the shortcut baseline).
- Has a results table and figures that hold up to questions from researchers.
- Has resume-ready descriptions of the work (written into the Notion board on each task).

Working agreement:
- Tasks are tracked on the Notion board "Derek". New work gets a task; when ready, the task
  gets a resume-style summary and moves to "Ready for Review"; the owner moves it to "Done".
- Changes land through pull requests the owner reviews.
